Geometry counts are real generated meshes; image crossfades demonstrate the transition only. They are not a tested native blending technique.

How distance controls detail

At a 1080-pixel viewport and 60° vertical field of view, pixels per metre = 935 / distance in metres. Choose the coarsest geometry whose cell-diagonal error projects to at most 3 pixels; budget pressure may require coarser detail. Full source detail is requested when a 2.5 m block spans at least 12 pixels. Hysteresis of 12% on distance and a 0.3 s transition reduce switching. These are tunable heuristics, not a proven mathematical optimum.

The game chooses LOD per model using thresholds, distance adjustments and hysteresis. Its selected model LOD is not exposed through normal ModAPI. This prototype follows those principles without claiming synchronization.

“Full” currently means the full block-bound source mesh, not the game's detailed model topology. Coarse cells can close openings or soften heat features. Original temperature samples are retained. The near-detail level preserves the closest quarter of source triangles for this fixed camera and merges the remainder. This is surface-level priority, not yet native per-block topology. The three views are separate inspection cameras; cross-grid occlusion, perspective and native performance are not implemented here.
